Event recap

DIGIS Workshop on AI Compliance

Robustness, Trustworthiness, Fairness

22 April 2026 09:30-13:30 Sala Stringa, FBK Povo

The workshop brought together FBK researchers and colleagues to map ongoing activities on AI compliance and to discuss how regulatory, technical and ethical requirements shape research on robust, trustworthy and fair AI systems.

Browse the programme Back to DIGIS

Workshop recap

A technical half-day dedicated to compliance in the digital society, with a particular focus on AI compliance and its impact on robustness, safety, fairness, reliability, accountability, contextual constraints and emerging regulation.

AI compliance

Standards, checklists and methods for trustworthy and responsible AI development.

Robust systems

Verification, auditing and lifecycle approaches for AI-based digital systems.

Fairness

Fairness, bias and accountability in datasets, models, LLMs and urban digital twins.

Collaboration

A multidisciplinary exchange across DIGIS units, FBK centres and research lines.
